BOOK KNOWLEDGE BASE: AUDIT OF THE DEPLOYED RECOMMENDATION ENGINE
====================================================================
source: WELLNESSHUB4/Book.md, section 56 (HARDCODED RECOMMENDATION LOGIC)

input grid enumerated                     : 378 (ages 18-80 x 2 sexes x 3 activity levels)

DISTINCT OUTPUTS THE ENGINE CAN PRODUCE
  distinct diet prescriptions             : 6
  distinct activity/sleep/water sets      : 1
  distinct full recommendations           : 14
  distinct once age-based HR zones added  : 198

  compression (outputs / inputs)          : 0.0370

COVERAGE
  inputs with a defined calorie target    : 19.0%
  note: book states the pattern continues but does not give values

INTERPRETATION
  The diet and activity components of the engine are a lookup table.
  Excluding the continuous heart-rate zones, which vary with age by
  arithmetic rather than by clinical reasoning, 378 distinct users
  receive one of only 14 distinct recommendations. Activity, sleep and
  water targets are population constants: 1 distinct value(s) across
  the whole grid. No logged behaviour enters the engine at any point.

  This is the L1 rung of the personalization ladder and the baseline the
  paper measures against. It is guideline-faithful and safe; the open
  question the paper answers is whether it is sufficient.
