THE PRIVACY-UTILITY CURVE
  rung                                     R2  marginal  what the user must disclose
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  L0 population constant              -0.0099      +nan  nothing
  L1 demographics (the book)          -0.0198   -0.0099  age and sex, once at signup
  L2 + body measurements              -0.0255   -0.0057  height, weight, waist
  L3 + own behaviour history          +0.2749   +0.3004  a daily activity log, ongoing
  L4 + yesterday's context            +0.2692   -0.0057  mood and location, passively and continuously

======================================================================================
HEADLINE
  L3 (a behaviour log) buys +0.3004 over body measurements.
  L4 (continuous passive sensing) buys a further -0.0057.

  The most intrusive layer is the one that returns almost
  nothing. On this evidence an application should collect a
  behaviour log and stop there.
